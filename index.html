SOL.SWx

Real-time space weather monitoring for iOS

SOL.SWx is an ad-free iOS app bringing live space weather data from NASA and NOAA to your fingertips. Track aurora forecasts and Kp index predictions, monitor real-time solar imagery from SDO, SOHO, STEREO, and GOES observatories, and receive alerts for X-ray flares and geomagnetic storms. Built for aurora chasers, radio operators, and anyone tracking solar activity.
